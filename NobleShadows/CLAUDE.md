## Usage discipline (always on)

* Be stingy with screenshots: one verification shot per phase gate, never one per step. Describe results in text instead.
* Summarize tool output; never paste raw dumps (scene hierarchies, logs, listings) into the conversation unless asked.
* Prefer targeted queries (one asset, one folder) over broad scans.
* Batch independent tool calls in a single block.
* Never poll or "keep checking until X" — use a fixed number of checks or a stop condition, then report.
* Keep chat replies concise: what you did, what changed, what's next. No play-by-play narration.
* Don't retry a failed expensive operation more than twice — stop and report.
* At WAIT FOR APPROVAL gates, end your turn cleanly. No speculative extra work while waiting.
* Never tail the editor log; check it once at completion, or on fixed intervals with a stop count — then report.
